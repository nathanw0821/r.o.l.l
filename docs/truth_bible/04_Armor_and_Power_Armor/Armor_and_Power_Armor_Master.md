## 🚜 2. Power Armor Hierarchy & Innate Chassis Math

### The Hidden Power Armor Multiplier:
Wearing any complete Power Armor frame automatically applies two **innate, hidden engine properties** before any piece DR is calculated:
1. **$42\%$ Flat Damage Reduction**: Every attack you receive is immediately reduced by $42\%$.
2. **$90\%$ Flat Radiation Reduction**: You take only one-tenth the radiation damage of an unarmored player.

### Power Armor Models Compared:

| Power Armor Model | Distinct Advantage | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Union Power Armor** | **$+150$ Poison Resistance** + **$+75$ Carry Weight** | **The Undisputed Best in Slot**. Completely ignores deadly Mirelurk Queen / Moonshine Jamboree acid spit. |
| **Hellcat Power Armor** | **$+12\%$ Innate Ballistic Reduction** ($2\%$ per piece) | Multiplicative ballistic tank. Best against armor-piercing ballistic bosses (Daily Ops piercing gaze). |
| **Excavator Power Armor** | **$+100$ Carry Weight** + $4\times$ Ore Mining Yield | The ultimate mining and scrap hauling workhorse. Budget-friendly, zero bullion required. |
| **T-65 Power Armor** | Highest raw paper DR/ER/RR (565 DR / 470 ER). | Looks formidable, but loses to Union's poison immunity and Hellcat's ballistic percentage reduction due to the 350 DR soft-cap. |
| **Strangler Heart** | Adds Acid Damage aura to nearby enemies and attacks. | Vault 94 raid relic. Fun for high-volume auto-heavy guns. |

## 🧬 3. Jetpack Mechanics & Torso Mods

* **Secret Service & Civil Engineer Chest**: Can equip a **Jetpack** without needing Power Armor!
* **Emergency Protocols (Power Armor)**: When health drops below $20\%$, increases movement speed by $25\%$ and reduces incoming damage by an additional **$50\%$**. The cornerstone of Low-Health Power Armor builds.
