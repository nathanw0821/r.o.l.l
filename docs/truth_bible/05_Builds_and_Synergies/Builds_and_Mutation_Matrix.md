# 🧬 Builds, Mutations & Food Buff Synergy Matrix
*The exact synergy mechanics that separate casual vault dwellers from Appalachian demigods.*

---

## 🩸 1. Build Archetype A: Bloodied Stealth Commando

* **Core Premise**: Operate continuously at **$19.9\%$ Health** (managing radiation using Toxic Sludge, Spoiled Meat, or Gamma Gun).
* **The Synergy Loop**:
  * **[[Unyielding Armor]] (x5)**: Grants $+3$ to all SPECIAL stats (except Endurance) per piece ➔ **$+15$ Strength, Perception, Charisma, Intelligence, Agility, Luck**.
  * **33 Luck Threshold**: Hits the golden crit ratio (Crit every alternating shot with Critical Savvy 3).
  * **High Agility (+15)**: Provides an enormous base Action Point pool and near-total stealth invisibility.
  * **High Intelligence (+15)**: Multiplies event XP gain by $+45\%$.
  * **[[Nerd Rage]] (Rank 3)**: Triggers at $<20\%$ HP ➔ $+20\%$ Additive Damage, $+40$ DR, and $+15\%$ AP Refresh.
  * **[[Serendipity]] (Rank 3)**: $45\%$ chance to completely ignore incoming damage while below $30\%$ HP.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Build Archetype B: Full-Health Immortal Heavy PA

* **Core Premise**: Maximize flat percentage mitigation while wielding high-capacity heavy weapons. Zero radiation maintenance.
* **The Synergy Loop**:
  * **[[Overeaters Power Armor]] (x5)**: $30\%$ flat damage reduction.
  * **[[What Rads?]] (Legendary Perk)**: Passively regenerates $+6$ HP and cures $+40$ Rads/second. Makes nuke zones walkable without radiation suits.
  * **[[Stabilized]] (Rank 3)**: Heavy guns ignore **$45\%$ target armor** and gain pinpoint accuracy while inside Power Armor.
  * **[[Electric Absorption]] (Legendary Perk)**: Energy attacks heal your character and recharge your Power Armor Fusion Core!
  * **Preferred Weapons**: [[Holy Fire]] (rapid Vampire healing), [[Cremator]] (massive AOE multi-shot DOT), [[Plasma Caster]] (sniper-level single shot crits).

## 🥗 3. The Herbivore vs. Carnivore Mutation War

You must choose either **Herbivore** or **Carnivore**; you cannot run both. With **Strange in Numbers** perk active on a public team, food buff effects are amplified by **$+25\%$**.

### Why Herbivore Dominates the V.A.T.S. Meta:
Herbivores gain double benefits from plant-based recipes, producing unmatched V.A.T.S. and XP multipliers:

| Food / Drink Buff | Base Effect | With Herbivore + Strange in Numbers | Recipe Source & Ease |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blight Soup** | $+20\%$ VATS Crit Damage | **$+125\%$ VATS Critical Damage** | Blight harvested easily around Ash Heap. Lasts 30 mins. |
| **Company Tea** | $+10\%$ AP Refresh Speed | **$+25\%$ Insane AP Regen** | Automated in C.A.M.P. Company Tea Machine. Lasts 60 mins. |
| **Brain Fungus Soup** | $+2$ Intelligence | **$+7.5$ Intelligence** ($+15\%$ XP) | Brain fungus found in Abandoned Waste Dump / Glassed Cavern. |
| **Cranberry Relish** | $+10\%$ Bonus XP | **$+25\%$ Flat Bonus XP** | Cranberries from Aaronholt Homestead or Cranberry Bog. |

> [!TIP]
> **Carnivore Use Case**: Carnivore is superior **only** for Melee builds, granting massive $+215\%$ melee damage buffs from Mutant Hound Stew and Yao Guai Roast, plus $+8$ Strength from Deathclaw Steaks.

## 🌀 4. Essential Mutations Roster

Never run mutations without **Class Freak (Rank 3)** (reduces negative mutation effects by $75\%$) and **Starched Genes (Rank 2)** (prevents RadAway from curing mutations):
* **Marsupial**: $+20$ Carry Weight and massive jump height.
* **Bird Bones**: Slower falling speed, $+4$ Agility.
* **Eagle Eyes**: $+50\%$ Critical Damage, $+4$ Perception.
* **Speed Demon**: $+20\%$ Movement speed and faster reload speed.
* **Adrenal Reaction**: Up to $+63\%$ bonus damage at low HP.
* **Herd Mentality**: $+2$ to all SPECIAL stats while on a team (even if on a public team alone!).
