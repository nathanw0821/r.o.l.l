## ⚡ 1. The Scrapping Algorithm & Probability Math

Random legendary rolling at the Purveyor or Tinkerer workbench has been **completely replaced** by the Milepost Zero Box Mod system. You now craft exact God-Rolls deterministically by socketing loose box mods.

### Scrapping Drop Probabilities:
When scrapping any 1★, 2★, or 3★ legendary item at a workbench:
* **Chance to Permanently Learn the Crafting Plan**: **$1.0\%$ per star** (e.g. Scrapping a 3★ weapon rolls three separate $1\%$ chances to permanently unlock the recipe).
* **Chance to Receive a Loose Box Mod**: **$1.5\%$ per star** (Drops an inventory item you can trade or socket immediately).
* **Scrip Yield**: Standard legendary scrip returned based on stars.

> [!TIP]
> **Scrapping Priority Strategy**: Never scrip items with S-tier stars (Quad, Bloodied, Overeaters, Explosive, 25% Less AP Cost). **Always scrap them at a workbench** until you permanently unlock the plan!

## 💎 2. Legendary Module Costs per Tier

Once you permanently unlock a recipe, crafting a Box Mod at the Tinkerer Workbench requires **Legendary Modules** + specific in-game bobbleheads/materials:

| Legendary Star Tier | Module Cost | Additional Crafting Ingredient | Tradeable? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1-Star ($\bigstar$)** | **15 Modules** | Specific Bobblehead (e.g., Small Guns for Quad, Leader for Bloodied) | Yes (Box Mod) |
| **2-Star ($\bigstar\bigstar$)** | **30 Modules** | Specific Material (e.g., Explosive Bobblehead, Faster Fire Rate) | Yes (Box Mod) |
| **3-Star ($\bigstar\bigstar\bigstar$)** | **45 Modules** | Hard Material (e.g., Pure Flux, Swift Bobblehead) | Yes (Box Mod) |
| **4-Star ($\bigstar\bigstar\bigstar\bigstar$)** | **60 Modules** | Raid Drops & Season 26 High-Tier Mats | Bound on Socket |

### Character Binding Rule:
* **Loose Box Mods**: 100% tradeable and sellable in player vendors.
* **Socketed Items**: The moment you apply a Box Mod to a weapon or armor piece, **that item becomes character-bound** and cannot be traded or dropped to other players.

## 🏆 3. Definitive Legendary Affix Tier List (Current Meta)

### Weapon Affixes:

#### 1-Star ($\bigstar$) Primary:
* **S-Tier**:
  * **[[Quad]]**: $+300\%$ ammo capacity (The DPS king for Commando, Railway, Tesla).
  * **[[Bloodied]]**: Up to $+95\%$ additive damage scaling inverse to HP.
  * **[[Anti-Armor]]**: Ignores $50\%$ target armor (Dominates bosses and high-DR targets).
  * **[[Vampire's]]**: Heals $2\%$ health per hit (God-tier for fast auto-melee and shotguns).
* **A-Tier**:
  * **Aristocrat's**: $+50\%$ damage at $>29,000$ caps.
  * **Two-Shot**: Adds 2nd projectile (meta for explosive launchers: Cryolator, AGL, Fat Man).
* **F-Tier (Instant Scrappers)**: Berserker's, Nocturnal, Ghoul Slayer's, Mutant Slayer's, Zealot's.

#### 2-Star ($\bigstar\bigstar$) Secondary:
* **S-Tier**:
  * **Rapid**: $+25\%$ Weapon Attack Speed / Fire Rate.
  * **Vital**: $+50\%$ Critical Damage.
  * **Explosive**: $+20\%$ area-of-effect damage (Applies explosive tagging).
  * **Heavy Hitter's**: $+40\%$ Power Attack Damage (Required for Auto-Axe and Chainsaw).

#### 3-Star ($\bigstar\bigstar\bigstar$) Tertiary:
* **S-Tier**:
  * **V.A.T.S. Optimized**: $-25\%$ Action Point Cost.
  * **Swift**: $+15\%$ Reload Speed.
  * **Durability**: $+50\%$ Break Slower (Crucial for Railway Rifles & Enclave Flamers).
  * **Strength**: $+3$ Strength (Melee scaling).
